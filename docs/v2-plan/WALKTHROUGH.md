assessment run and one practice drill of each station, then fill this in. Anything marked here is
either fixed before submission or listed under Limitations in the report.

## How to run it

1. Start the app (`npm run dev`) and sign in.
2. Open **Simulator** in the navigation.
3. **Assessment:** choose "Assessment: the full circuit", then Start assessment.
4. **Practice:** pick a station card, or choose Practice on the simulator's first panel and pick a
   station. "Restart station" puts the car back at the station's start.

**Keys:** W accelerate, S brake, A and D steer, R to change between D and R (only when stopped),
Q to look over your left shoulder (the blind spot), E to look down out of your window (on the hill, the yellow band shows beside the door mirror),
drag the view to look anywhere, including over your right shoulder,
Space for the handbrake, T for the top-down view, P or Esc to pause.

**On a phone:** hold it sideways. The left pad steers and keeps its angle; double-tap it to centre.
The right pedals press harder the higher up you touch them. The buttons along the bottom are D/R,
handbrake, pause and the top-down view. If the touch controls do not appear, open Settings on the
simulator's first panel and set Touch controls to On.
